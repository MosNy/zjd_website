Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our services

How do I start a project with you?

To start a project, simply contact us via the form, by email or by phone. We will then arrange a free consultation to discuss your needs and objectives.

How long does it take to develop an application?

Development time varies depending on the complexity of the project. An MVP can take 4-8 weeks, while a full application may require 3-6 months. We establish a precise timeline during the planning phase.

Do you offer maintenance services?

Yes, we offer maintenance and support services for all our projects. We offer different maintenance packages adapted to your needs and budget.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely! With offices in Dubai, France and Turkey, we work with clients from around the world. Our multilingual team facilitates international communication.
